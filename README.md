## 🚀 Flagship Project

### 🤖 Multi-Agent AI Research Assistant

**AI-powered research and report generation using n8n**

An intelligent multi-agent workflow designed to automate the research process. It breaks complex queries into smaller topics, gathers information, summarizes findings and generates structured research reports.

**Core Features**

- 🧠 Planner Agent – Breaks queries into research subtopics.
- 🔍 Research Agent – Collects information for each topic.
- 📝 Summarizer – Creates concise research summaries.
- 📄 Report Generator – Organizes findings into reports.
- ✅ Critic Agent – Reviews generated reports.

**Technology:** n8n | Agentic AI | Prompt Engineering | LLMs
